Miso Tracker priacy policy

Effective date: February 25, 2026

Miso Tracker is a life tracking app for Magic: The Gathering. It is designed to work entirely offline and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data collection

Miso Tracker does not collect any personal information. The app does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any other third-party services.

Data storage

All app data (such as life totals, player count, and preferences) is stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Data sharing

Miso Tracker does not share any data with anyone, for any purpose.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this same URL with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact hello@misotracker.com
